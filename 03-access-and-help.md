### What participants told us

At the community workshop in Oxford on 12 November 2026, arts and humanities researchers, data services and support staff voted on the challenges that limit their use of AI. This episode responds to:

- **C2** Don't know what tools or computing are available or how to get them (7 votes)
- No institutional affiliation or short-term contracts (2 votes)
- **S2** No specialist to work with (3 votes)
