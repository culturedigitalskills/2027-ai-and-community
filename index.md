This lesson is for arts and humanities researchers who want to use AI but are unsure what it involves behind the scenes. It is also for the librarians, data service staff and research support teams who help them. No technical background is needed.

The lesson is an output of the [Connecting Arts and Humanities Infrastructures for AI Capability](https://artsandhumanities.ai) project funded by UKRI under IS8.

By the end of the lesson learners would learn the basis of AI infrastructure, including:

- what are data centres?
- where their data goes when they use AI tools?
- what they can access?
- what are the costs, including time and environmental impact?
- who can help?

Hands-on exercises help them make informed, responsible choices about AI infrastructure.


It is written for arts and humanities researchers, data services and support staff with no technical background 

The lesson has five short episodes of about 25 minutes each (10 minutes teaching and 15 minutes exercises). Each episode stands alone, so they can be taught separately or in any order.
